Research Interests

My research primarily focuses on combinatorial commutative algebra, particularly the algebraic properties of edge ideals associated with weighted and unweighted graphs. I am interested in understanding associated primes, Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity, and related homological properties of monomial ideals.
